
The **Moxi robot**, developed by Diligent Robotics, is an **AI-powered mobile manipulator** designed to serve as a clinical assistant in hospitals.1 Its primary purpose is to **automate non-patient-facing logistical tasks** that consume a significant portion of a nurse's time, thus helping to reduce staff burnout and allowing clinical personnel to focus more on direct patient care.2

---

## Moxi's Function and Operations

Moxi is essentially an errand-runner and logistics assistant for hospital staff.3

### Core Tasks

Moxi specializes in taking over time-consuming, low-level tasks, which can free up to **30% of a nurse's time** spent on "fetching and gathering."4 These tasks include:

- **Delivering Lab Samples:** Transporting specimens to the lab.5
    
- **Medication Delivery:** Picking up medications, including discharge meds and specialized drugs like oncology medication, from the pharmacy.6
    
- **Supply Transport:** Running patient supplies, fetching items from central supply (like blankets or IV poles), and distributing **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**.7
    
- **Logistical Support:** Delivering after-hours patient meals and transferring equipment like telemetry boxes.8
    

### Technical Functionality

The robot uses advanced technology to operate autonomously in complex hospital environments:9

- **Navigation:** Moxi uses an AI system, combined with sensors (like front-facing camera and rear-facing lidar), to learn and navigate the hospital's layout, hallways, and elevators.10 It can also use data to avoid routes or elevators that are always busy.11
    
- **Manipulation:** It is equipped with a **compliant arm and a gripper hand** which allows it to interact with the environment.12 This dexterity enables it to open various doors, including those requiring **badge access**, and press elevator buttons autonomously.13
    
- **Human Interaction:** Moxi is designed with **social intelligence** and features like heart-shaped eyes and beeping sounds to interact in a friendly way with staff and patients.14 Clinical staff can request Moxi via a **hospital-issued phone** (text or call).15
    
- **Delivery:** It has **secure, locked compartments (drawers)** to safely transport sensitive items like medications and lab samples.16

## Case Study Highlights (Mary Washington Healthcare)

The implementation of Moxi robots has shown significant positive results in various hospitals, such as Mary Washington Healthcare (MWHC) and Northwestern Memorial Hospital.

### Impact Metrics

At MWHC, for instance, the deployment of Moxi robots between February 2022 and April 2023 yielded compelling results:

- **Time Savings:** Over **14,300 hours** of time were returned to nurses and clinical teams.
    
- **Deliveries Completed:** Over **27,500 deliveries** were completed.
    
- **Physical Savings:** Moxi robots saved clinical staff over **13,600,000 steps** (approximately 4,600 miles) of walking.
    
- **Efficiency:** The pharmacy department was Moxi's highest user, with over 8,600 deliveries, saving an average of 26 minutes per task.
    

### Implementation and Staff Acceptance

The successful integration of Moxi at sites like MWHC was attributed to:

- **Staff Buy-in:** Nursing leadership engaged with bedside nurses early to determine which workflows Moxi should take over, ensuring the robot would support, not replace, indispensable staff.
    
- **Seamless Integration:** Implementation required **no infrastructure additions or upgrades** to the hospital, connecting over the existing Wi-Fi network.17
    
- **Positive Morale:** Staff often view Moxi as a **teammate** that eases stress and workload.18 The robot has become popular among staff, improving morale and even posing for selfies.19
