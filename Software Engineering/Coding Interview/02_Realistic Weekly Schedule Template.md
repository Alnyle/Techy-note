## 🛠️ Practical Study Methods for Your Situation

### 1. **Integrated Learning**
- Connect CS study topics with your college courses
- Apply algorithms to your graduation project problems
- Use spaced repetition during natural breaks

```
Digital Flashcards (Anki or similar):
- Time complexities
- Algorithm steps

Paper/Whiteboard:
- Algorithm implementations
- Data structure diagrams
- System design sketches
```

### 3. **Graduation Project Integration**

- Use project work as "practical application" time

## 🚨 Realistic Expectations & Adjustments

### During Exam Periods
- Reduce CS study to 30 mins/day (flashcard review only)
- Focus on exams, then resume normal schedule
- Don't feel guilty about pausing - this is normal!

### When Graduation Project Demands More Time
- Scale back to maintenance mode (flashcards only)
- Schedule "catch-up" weekends after major milestones
- Remember: Your graduation project IS relevant experience

### Burnout Prevention
- One full rest day per week
- If overwhelmed, focus on quality over quantity
- 30 minutes of focused study > 2 hours of distracted study

## 💡 Key Success Factors for Your Situation

1. **Consistency Over Intensity**: Regular short sessions beat occasional marathons
2. **Integration**: Connect study topics with college and project work
3. **Flexibility**: Adjust based on academic demands
4. **Spaced Repetition**: Use flashcards to combat forgetting during busy periods
5. **Practical Application**: Implement what you learn in your graduation project
