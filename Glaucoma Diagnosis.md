
[Research](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaophthalmology/fullarticle/2815035):**GPT-4 demonstrates superior diagnostic skills in eye care**  
Researchers showed that GPT-4 can match or even exceed the diagnostic capabilities of human eye specialists. The study compared GPT-4’s responses with those of 12 attending specialists and three senior trainees. The evaluation covered a diverse set of 20 ophthalmology questions and 20 patient cases, focusing on glaucoma and retina disorders, to assess the AI's accuracy and completeness of responses. The study revealed that GPT-4's responses were more comprehensive than those of the human specialists, particularly in the field of glaucoma.
